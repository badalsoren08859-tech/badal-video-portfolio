About Me

I create research-driven documentaries on systemic and social issues affecting tribal communities, particularly Santhali communities in Jharkhand and Bengal. Each video combines original research, face-cam storytelling, and high-quality animation to expose injustice and celebrate tribal culture.

I handle all aspects of production—research and fact-checking, scriptwriting, filming, After Effects animation, Premiere Pro editing, and thumbnail design. My work follows the explanation-plus-evidence format, inspired by documentaries that matter.
