About Me

I'm Badal, a documentary filmmaker dedicated to telling stories that matter. I create research-driven documentaries on systemic and social issues affecting tribal communities, particularly the Santhali communities in Jharkhand and Bengal.

From initial research and scriptwriting to post-production animation and editing, I handle every aspect of the production process. My work combines evidence-based storytelling with compelling visuals to expose injustice and celebrate tribal culture.
